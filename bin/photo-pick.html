browsing:

Timeline order, corrected. Click a frame to select it, ← → to move, k keep · b blog · x discard · u unset. Marks overwrite each other freely, so to change your mind just press another key — marking advances the cursor, so press ← first to redo the one you just did. Enter or double-click opens the full-size original; Esc closes it. Keep and blog both go to the archive.org item; blog additionally gets a 2000px avif. Discards shrink but stay put, so u can always reach them; they only move to img/.trash/ on Apply, and are never deleted. A dashed border with ~ means the time is extrapolated past that camera's anchors, so treat its position as approximate.
