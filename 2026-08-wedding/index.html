We got the outside lawn & gardens, the seven upstairs rooms, dining room, the pub, the "Snug", and an extra "Conservatory" room that's almost like a greehouse. An utterly beautiful, faithfully constructed, English Inn and Pub, offering candlelit dining for every meal, true Old World charm, and beautful decor. Karin, my friend & bridesmaid, had similar enough hair and is a braiding wizard. _Thankfully_ she agreed to do my hair and set up my fascinator / adorbs lil' veil hat. 🙏🏽 [![bride getting ready](../img/wed-canon/IMG_6836.avif)](https://archive.org/serve/pelican-wedding/wed-canon/IMG_6836.heic.jpg) Doing final prep, trying to avoid the jitters. _Thankfully_ myself and a bunch of the biking crew did a great redwoods + coastal mountain/gravel bike ride _that morning_ to help with endorophins and stress :p [![bride doing makeup in mirror](../img/wed-canon/IMG_6844.avif)](https://archive.org/serve/pelican-wedding/wed-canon/IMG_6844.heic.jpg) Bridesmaids!
