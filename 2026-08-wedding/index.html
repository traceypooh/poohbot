[![wedding couple, english inn, grass, flowers](../img/wed/DSC_9751.avif)](https://archive.org/serve/pelican-wedding/wed/DSC_9751.jpg) These are my three favorite pictures -- Drew from our biking squad got this one: [![wedding couple side profile](../img/wed-misc/drew-side.avif)](https://archive.org/serve/pelican-wedding/wed-misc/drew-side.heic) ## The Story Karin, my friend & bridesmaid, had similar enough hair and is a braiding wizard. _Thankfully_ she agreed to do my hair and set up my fascinator / adorbs lil' veil hat. 🙏🏽 [![xxx](../img/wed-canon/IMG_6836.avif)](https://archive.org/serve/pelican-wedding/wed-canon/IMG_6836.heic.jpg) Doing final prep, trying to avoid the jitters. _Thankfully_ myself and a bunch of the biking crew did a great redwoods + coastal mountain/gravel bike ride _that morning_ to help with endorophins and stress :p [![xxx](../img/wed-canon/IMG_6844.avif)](https://archive.org/serve/pelican-wedding/wed-canon/IMG_6844.heic.jpg) The boys have "snapped" (snapping to the beautiful live Hunter's brass music downbeats) their way down the aisle -- we are about to go on. Katherine has already started down the aisle; Tara is pictured in the background. I had *just* realized I forgot Hunter's ring upstairs and ran and got it. MY DRESS HAS POCKETS! [![xxx](../img/wed-misc/selfie-bridesmaids2.jpg)](https://archive.org/serve/pelican-wedding/wed-misc/selfie-bridesmaids.jpg) OMG. Walking into next room (the "Conservatory"), about to go on, I saw the cake setup for the first time and started to tear up - it was so pretty! [![xxx](../img/wed-misc/selfie-cake.jpg)](https://archive.org/serve/pelican-wedding/wed-misc/selfie-cake.jpg) We wrote heartfelt custom vows. The dress pockets had my vows, phone, and his ring. I spent most of the Ceremony trying not to cry -- once the vows started, I was fine and really enjoying the Event. I knew the rest of the day was going to be *perfect* and so much fun spending time with friends and family. [![xxx](../img/wed/DSC_9573.avif)](https://archive.org/serve/pelican-wedding/wed/DSC_9573.JPG) Sliding on Hunter's ring. Neither of us had any hint of what the other was getting them. We both **loved** our rings! 💍 [![xxx](../img/wed/DSC_5510.avif)](https://archive.org/serve/pelican-wedding/wed/DSC_5510.JPG) Mike Wood, our Officiant, and leader of our biking group, did *SUCH* an amazing job. His dad was an Episcopal Bishop -- so he was able to channel some of his dad's spirit beautifully. Mike really made our day Special - huge Thanks!! 🙏🏽 [![xxx](../img/wed/DSC_9603long.avif)](https://archive.org/serve/pelican-wedding/wed/DSC_9603long.jpg) We did it! We're married! 🎉 [![xxx](../img/wed/DSC_9605.avif)](https://archive.org/serve/pelican-wedding/wed/DSC_9605.JPG)
